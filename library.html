Before you visit

  1. Plan your visit.Bring your student identification and a list of the topics you want to explore.
  2. Ask for guidance.Check with library staff about available materials, study areas, and borrowing rules.
  3. Care for shared resources.Keep noise low and return borrowed materials according to the confirmed library policy.

Common questions

How do I borrow a book?

Ask the library team about registration, borrowing limits, and return dates. Bring your student identification.

Can I study with a group?

Ask staff which areas allow group discussions so quiet study spaces remain comfortable for everyone.
